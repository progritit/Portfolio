01 · About

Technical curiosity, grounded in professional experience.

I am building toward full-stack JavaScript development through The Odin Project, with hands-on experience creating modular applications, working with APIs, managing application state, testing behaviour, and structuring maintainable front-end code.

My development practice now goes beyond isolated exercises: I use modern tooling such as Webpack, npm, ESLint, Prettier, Jest, and Git to build, test, refactor, and deploy complete projects. Recent work has also strengthened my understanding of asynchronous JavaScript, data structures, code organisation, and application architecture.

Before transitioning into development, I built a multidisciplinary background across economics, data analysis, training administration, business operations, and process coordination. That experience helps me approach software with both technical structure and practical business context in mind.

I use AI intentionally as a learning, debugging, and problem-solving partner while keeping ownership of the concepts, architecture, and code I build. My goal is to develop useful digital products with clarity, maintainability, and real-world purpose.
